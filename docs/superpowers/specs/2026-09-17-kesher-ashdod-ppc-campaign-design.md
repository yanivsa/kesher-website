# Kesher — PPC Campaign Design: Couples Counseling in Ashdod

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: approved direction / production design, pending implementation review
Primary URL: `/couples-counseling-ashdod`
Repository: `yanivsa/kesher-website`

## 1. Objective

Turn high-intent Google Search traffic for couples counseling in Ashdod into paid booked consultations.

Primary KPI: `booking_complete` / cost per booked consultation.
Secondary diagnostic events: `booking_start`, `whatsapp_click`, `phone_click`, primary CTA clicks, scroll depth.

Do not optimize the program around CTR alone.

## 2. Audience

Primary audience: adults in Ashdod or regularly in Ashdod who are actively searching for couples counseling and experiencing recurring conflict, withdrawal, distance, or conversations that repeatedly end in the same place.

Primary initiator: the partner who has reached the point of searching for help.

Secondary decision-maker / veto player: the partner who may worry that counseling will become a trial, that someone will take sides, or that one meeting automatically commits the couple to a long process.

## 3. Core problem and desired outcome

Core problem: the topic of the disagreement changes, but the interaction pattern repeats.

Desired outcome: understand the repeated interaction pattern, reduce escalation, and leave the first meeting with one practical direction to try.

This campaign does not promise to save a relationship, restore love, or guarantee a result.

## 4. Offer

- 50-minute couples consultation.
- Clinic in Ashdod or online via Zoom.
- 500 ILS including VAT.
- No advance commitment to a fixed number of sessions.
- Primary CTA: `קביעת פגישה`.
- Secondary CTA: `יש לי שאלה לפני שקובעים` via WhatsApp.

## 5. Single-minded message

**כשכל שיחה חוזרת לאותו מקום, אפשר להתחיל לדבר אחרת.**

Supporting promise:

`מזהים מה קורה ביניכם כשהשיחה מסתבכת, נותנים מקום לשתי נקודות המבט ומתחילים לתרגל דרך מעשית אחרת לנהל את השיחה.`

## 6. Approved creative direction

### Big Idea — "לצאת מהלופ"

The campaign reframes the problem from "we fight about many things" to "the topic changes, but the conversation pattern repeats".

Persuasion sequence:

1. Recognition — "שוב אותה שיחה, שוב אותו מקום."
2. Reframe — the recurring pattern is the useful thing to examine.
3. Mechanism — identify the pattern, notice where escalation starts, practice a different response.
4. Objection handling — no search for blame and no choosing sides.
5. Low-friction offer — start with one structured 50-minute meeting.
6. CTA — book a meeting.

## 10. Search campaign production

### Campaign

`Search | Couples Counseling | Ashdod`

Location: Ashdod only, using presence / regularly-in targeting rather than interest-in-location targeting.

Initial match strategy: Exact + Phrase. Do not start with broad match before sufficient conversion data exists.

### Initial keyword clusters

High-intent counseling terms:

- `[ייעוץ זוגי אשדוד]`
- `"ייעוץ זוגי אשדוד"`
- `[ייעוץ זוגי באשדוד]`
- `"ייעוץ זוגי באשדוד"`
- `[יועצת זוגית אשדוד]`
- `"יועצת זוגית אשדוד"`
- `[ייעוץ נישואין אשדוד]`
- `"ייעוץ נישואין אשדוד"`

Do not initially bid on `טיפול זוגי` if the landing page and professional positioning remain explicitly counseling rather than psychotherapy. Product accuracy takes priority over raw search volume.

### Initial negative themes

- free / חינם
- studies, course, certification, jobs / לימודים, קורס, הכשרה, דרושים
- psychologist / פסיכולוג, פסיכולוגית
- lawyer / עורך דין, עורכת דין
- divorce legal services / גירושין משפטי
- mediation searches / גישור, מגשר when campaign intent is counseling
- informational-only terms such as worksheet, book, academic paper when they appear in search-term data

Negative-keyword decisions must be based on actual search-term reports after launch; do not over-block pre-emptively.

## 18. Measurement and implementation audit

Existing strengths:

- `variant_id` is already propagated into landing-page analytics.
- `booking_start` and `booking_complete` exist.
- WhatsApp and phone clicks are conversion events.
- Calendly passes attribution fields and booking context.

Issues to verify before paid launch:

1. Direct Google Ads conversion reporting currently sends `value: 1` even when `booking_complete` carries `value: 500`. Confirm whether production uses GTM/imported GA4 conversions or direct gtag conversion destinations. Fix only after identifying the active measurement mode so value is not double-counted or overwritten.
2. Confirm `booking_complete`, `whatsapp_click`, and `phone_click` are the intended Google Ads primary/secondary conversions and that no duplicate import exists.
3. Validate UTM/GCLID persistence in a real production booking flow.
4. Validate the A/B variant parameter survives attribution and does not cause duplicate indexing; canonical should stay on the clean URL.
5. Validate mobile Calendly layout and conversion-event firing after brand-color change.

## 19. Google policy constraints

Relationship counseling falls under Google's sensitive-interest category for personalized advertising (`Relationship hardships`).

Therefore:

- Search intent targeting is the primary channel.
- Do not create advertiser-curated remarketing/custom audiences based on relationship hardship behavior.
- Do not imply that the advertiser knows the user's private relationship status.
- Keep ad and landing copy factual and non-exploitative.

## 20. Agency review

Audience fit: strong — high-intent local search and the biggest veto objection are both addressed.

Message clarity: strong — one main message, one mechanism, one primary CTA.

Brand consistency: strong after the Calendly cyan is corrected.

Product accuracy: requires removal or verification of `יועצת נישואין מוסמכת`.

Visual continuity: strong if approved Shira references are used and clichés are excluded.

Platform fit: RSA assets are within current Google limits; organic/video assets can be repurposed after platform-specific review.

Claim accuracy: no performance promises or fabricated proof.

CTA clarity: booking primary, WhatsApp secondary.

AI-artifact risk: controlled by reference-based identity preservation and negative constraints; any generated human image must be manually reviewed before use.

Unnecessary complexity: avoid simultaneous multi-variant testing at low volume. A vs one challenger at a time.

## 21. Testing hypotheses

H1 — Recognition-led hero (A) will produce more booked consultations than generic service-led copy because it matches the user's lived problem while retaining the search keyword.

H2 — No-blame challenger (B) will improve conversions among hesitant users by reducing fear of judgment.

H3 — One-meeting challenger (C) will improve decision-stage conversion by reducing perceived commitment and making price/time explicit.

H4 — displaying 500 ILS above the fold may reduce raw CTA clicks but increase booking-start quality and booking-complete rate.

H5 — moving the partner-resistance block earlier may improve downstream booking engagement, but should be tested after the hero baseline is established.

## 22. Recommended production order

1. Apply non-test brand/accuracy repairs: credential wording and Calendly color.
2. Replace Variant A with approved control copy.
3. Map B and C to the two approved challengers but leave A as the default.
4. Run repository quality gate and landing-page mobile/desktop checks.
5. Verify analytics and Google Ads conversion configuration.
6. Build RSA and assets in Google Ads as draft only.
7. Human review of campaign location, budget, bids, keywords, negatives, conversion goals, and ad copy.
8. Launch only after explicit approval.
9. After enough conversion volume, test B against A, then C or structural changes.
10. Produce/approve visual assets and organic social content in parallel; publish only with explicit approval.

## 23. Human approval gates

Explicit approval is required before:

- merging changes that publish to production if the merge triggers deployment;
- launching or enabling Google Ads;
- setting or increasing paid-media spend;
- publishing social posts;
- using generated images of Shira;
- using testimonials or customer proof;
- changing conversion-goal configuration in Google Ads.

## 24. Items not to execute without additional evidence

- unsupported credentials;
- testimonials / reviews not supplied and approved;
- claims about success rate or typical outcomes;
- budget recommendations presented as data-driven before Keyword Planner / account auction data is inspected.
