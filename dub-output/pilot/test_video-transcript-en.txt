Sometimes the fight at home looks like it is about homework, screens, bedtime, or a child who does not listen.

But underneath the argument, there is often a deeper question: do the parents feel that they are on the same side?

When one parent becomes the police officer and the other becomes the rescuer, the child learns to move between them. The home becomes louder, the couple becomes more distant, and everyone feels alone.

The change begins when the parents stop arguing about who is right and start asking what kind of home they want to build together.

A calmer boundary does not start with a stronger voice. It starts with a shared decision.
