# Kesher attention & executive-function parenting workflow

This workflow supports parenting around daily functioning. It does not require an ADHD diagnosis and must not infer one.

## Safety and medical boundary

Do not use for ADHD diagnosis, symptom scoring, medication choice/dose/change, medical treatment, self-harm, violence, emergency situations, legal rights, benefits or school accommodations.

## Privacy

Do not send diagnosis status, medication, medical history, child name, exact age, school, raw story or transcript. Translate the request into age band, functional challenge, goal and support level only.

## Tool choice

Use `get_adhd_parenting_plan` when the core problem is starting, remembering, organizing, shifting, regulating frustration or building external supports. Use `get_parenting_response_plan` when the issue is better described as a general parenting interaction. Use `find_kesher_resource` only on an explicit request for Kesher content.

## Output rule

Normalize the need for external structure without making a medical claim. Offer environmental supports and caregiver actions. Include the tool's professional-support note without presenting it as diagnosis or treatment advice.
