🎯 **What:** Hardened the weekday article generation prompt and content validation script to prevent near-duplicate topics and overlapping content.

⚠️ **Risk:** The previous article generation could sometimes pick a topic that was too similar to a recently published post, leading to redundant content.

🛡️ **Solution:**
1. Updated the `.github/workflows/jules-weekday-article.yml` prompt to explicitly instruct the agent to check the 30 most recent articles in `src/data/posts.json` and ensure the new topic is substantially fresh.
2. Hardened `scripts/validate-content.cjs` to enforce this rule programmatically. The script now calculates the intersection of significant words between the new post's ID/slug and those of the 30 previous posts. If 2 or more significant words overlap, the validation fails, preventing the PR from being merged and requiring a fresh topic.
