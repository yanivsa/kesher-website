## Research/accuracy
Columbus Group (1991) defined giftedness through asynchronous development; Silverman was a founder, not the sole author. This is a conceptual description, not a diagnostic tool.
Some gifted children develop cognitive, social, emotional and motor domains unevenly. Not every gifted child struggles emotionally or socially. NAGC May 2025 notes most research does not establish that giftedness causes adverse social-emotional outcomes.
