## Cannibalization principles

- Separate URLs by user goal, not by arbitrary wording or arbitrary date counts.
- `/services/couples` = general/online couples counseling.
- `/couples-counseling-ashdod` = local Ashdod conversion page.
- Early-dating intentions and early-relationship pacing remain distinct problems.
- Money-conflict content must stay distinct from premarital financial-expectations content.
- Parent-boundary content and parent-yelling/self-regulation content should cross-link but not collapse into one page.

## Phase 4 editorial constraints

- No fabricated testimonials, case studies, quotes or statistics.
- No unverified claims that online counseling is equally effective as in-person counseling.
- No medical diagnosis/treatment positioning.
- Preserve existing page intent when updating.
- Use authoritative sources where factual/health claims require support.
- Keep direct answers concise and useful for both search and AI retrieval.
- Do not generate new URLs outside the revised Wave 1.
